# Readie

A universal reading copilot that works on any text displayed on your screen—research papers, PDFs, web articles, or technical docs. Simply highlight text or ask questions in natural language to get clear explanations, summaries, or deep dives into complex ideas. It builds contextual understanding by referencing related research papers through RAG, helping you grasp advanced ML concepts and connect them to broader literature—all seamlessly on top of your current reading view.
